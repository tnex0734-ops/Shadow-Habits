# 🧠 108 UX Laws for a Habit Tracker App (Notion-Style)

Tailored specifically for building a **habit tracker / second-brain / productivity app**.

---

# 🔥 CORE DECISION & INTERACTION LAWS

## 1. Hick’s Law

Reduce number of habits shown at once → avoid overwhelm.

## 2. Fitts’s Law

Make “Complete Habit” button large & thumb-friendly.

## 3. Jakob’s Law

Use familiar patterns (checkboxes, streak counters like GitHub/Notion).

## 4. Miller’s Law

Limit visible habits to ~5–9 per screen.

## 5. Tesler’s Law

Hide complexity (analytics, configs) behind toggles.

## 6. Parkinson’s Law

Use timers / streak urgency to drive completion.

## 7. Goal Gradient Effect

Progress bars → users push harder near completion.

## 8. Zeigarnik Effect

Show incomplete habits prominently.

## 9. Peak-End Rule

End day with a satisfying “You completed X habits 🎉”.

## 10. Feedback Principle

Instant check animation on completion.
